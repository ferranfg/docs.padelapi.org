<Info>
  **Padel Fan Index** measures the real popularity of professional padel players among active padel fans. It is available through the API with `GET /api/players/{id}/audience`, in [Padel Analyst](/padel-analyst) with the `show-player-audience` tool, and as custom reports for brands, sponsors and players.
</Info>

## Why It's Different

Traditional sponsorship metrics rely on passive impressions: social media followers, TV viewership estimates, or event attendance. Padel Fan Index measures **active fan behaviour**: which players fans look up and which ones they pick for their teams, inside a game built around the professional tour.

Both signals come from people who follow the circuit closely, and both are compared with the player's competitive level. That separates the players fans care about from the ones who simply win.

## Audience Endpoint

`GET /api/players/{id}/audience` returns the popularity of any player over any period: how much of the fans' attention goes to them, where that places them on the circuit, whether that is more or less than their level would predict, and how it evolves.

<Note>
  This endpoint [requires a **Business** subscription](https://padelapi.org/billing).
</Note>

Every value is relative: shares, indexes and positions against the rest of the circuit. The endpoint never returns absolute fan counts, so values compare fairly across players and over time.

The endpoint measures a window: the last 12 months by default, ending today. Set `after_date` and `before_date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) to measure any other window. Every value in the response is computed over that same window.

```bash
curl 'https://padelapi.org/api/players/465/audience' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_TOKEN'
```

```json
{
  "id": 465,
  "self": "/api/players/465/audience",
  "from": "2025-11-19",
  "to": "2026-11-18",
  "fan_share": 2.6,
  "popularity_rank": 14,
  "popularity_index": 4.33,
  "by_period": [
    { "from": "2026-08-01", "to": "2026-08-31", "fan_share": 5.8 },
    { "from": "2026-09-01", "to": "2026-09-30", "fan_share": 3.6 },
    { "from": "2026-10-01", "to": "2026-10-31", "fan_share": 3.1 },
    { "from": "2026-11-01", "to": "2026-11-18", "fan_share": 5.2 }
  ],
  "trend": "rising",
  "connections": {
    "player": "/api/players/465"
  }
}
```

### Popularity

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `from`, `to` | The window actually measured, so you know what the defaults resolved to. |
| `fan_share` | Percentage of the fans' attention that went to the player over the window: of every time a fan looked up a professional player, the share that was this one. |
| `popularity_rank` | Position of the player on the circuit by `fan_share`. |
| `popularity_index` | Fan interest against the interest expected for the player's competitive level. `1.0` is exactly what their level predicts, `2.0` twice as much, `0.5` half. |

`fan_share` answers how followed a player is, and `popularity_rank` where that places them. `popularity_index` answers a different question: whether fans follow them more or less than their results alone would explain. A world-class player can be among the most followed on the circuit and still sit near or below `1.0`, because their level already predicts a great deal of attention, while a player outside the top 20 with an index of `3.0` is drawing far more interest than their ranking suggests.

The index combines two signals, how often fans look the player up and how often they pick them, each one compared with players of the same level. A player needs enough attention within the window for the index to be measured.

`fan_share` and `popularity_rank` are `null` when fans paid no attention to the player within the window, and `popularity_index` is also `null` when the sample is too small to measure it.

### Evolution

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `by_period` | One entry per calendar month within the window, oldest first, each with its `from` and `to` dates. The first and last entries are trimmed to the window. Months where fans paid no attention to the player are left out. |
| `by_period[].fan_share` | Percentage of the fans' attention that went to the player within that month. |
| `trend` | Direction of `fan_share` across `by_period`: `rising`, `stable` or `declining`. It is relative to the player's own level, so a move from 2% to 3% counts as much as one from 20% to 30%. `null` when fewer than three periods are available. |

## Data Sources

Padel Fan Index combines two proprietary data sources:
