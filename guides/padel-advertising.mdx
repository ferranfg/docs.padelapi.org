## The placement

Sponsored placement is a single native ad unit. It looks like content, not a banner: a small, friendly block with a short message and a link.

There is one slot per page, shared and rotated across advertisers, so it never turns the page into an ad wall. Your product shows up in a clean, calm space that people actually read.
