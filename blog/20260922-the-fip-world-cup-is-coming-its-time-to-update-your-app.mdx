**Hello developers,**

The **FIP World Cup** is in Doha from **2 to 7 November**: sixteen nations per category, six days and two titles. It is the best week of the year to have a padel app, and the Padel API is ready for it.

If you already read tournaments and matches from the API, adapting is a small job. Three things to know.

## 1. How it is played

The entrants are teams, not pairs. They are drawn into groups and play a round robin, and then the whole field moves into a knockout. Every meeting between two teams, from the first day to the final, is played as **three pair matches**, and the first team to win two of them wins the meeting. The third one is not played when the first two have already decided it.

You can tell these tournaments apart with the new **format** field: `pairs` on everything you read today, `teams` on these. One field, one branch, and the rest of your calendar is untouched.

## 2. Draws and ties

Each phase of the event is a value of the `draw` filter you already use for qualification: `?draw=group` gives you the round robin, `?draw=main` the knockout for the title, quarterfinals to final. Add `?draw=placement` if you also want the ties played for the positions below the podium.

Inside a team tournament every match brings a new `tie` object saying which meeting it belongs to:

```json
"tie": { "group": "A", "team_1": "AR", "team_2": "ES", "rubber": 1 }
```

The three matches of one meeting share `draw`, `round`, `index` and `category`, and `rubber` orders them. Group by those four fields, count the winners, and you have the result between the two teams.

## 3. What changes

Three details worth a look before November:

- A match can now come back as **`not_played`**, with both pairs on the sheet and no result, when the third one no longer decides anything. Handle it next to `bye` and `walkover`.
- Winning the final match is not winning the title. The final is three matches and the title goes to the team that takes two, so a pair can lose its match and still be champion.
- `winners` on the tournament holds the three pairs of the winning team, six players per category instead of two.

[Read the Team Tournaments guide](https://padelapi.org/docs/guides/team-tournaments)

You do not have to wait until November to test any of this. The 2024 edition is already in the API, complete with its group stage and every knockout tie, as **tournament `258`**:

```bash
curl 'https://padelapi.org/api/tournaments/258/matches?draw=all&per_page=50' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_TOKEN'
```

Point your integration at it today and you will be ready before the first ball is hit in Doha.
