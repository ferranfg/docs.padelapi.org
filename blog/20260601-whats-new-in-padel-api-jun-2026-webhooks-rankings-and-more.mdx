**Hello developers,**

Three big additions since our last post, all focused on giving you more data and less plumbing. Here's what's new:

## 1. Official ranking & RACE in a single call

You can now load the **full official ranking** and the **RACE to the Finals** with a single request, and pull the **history** of any player to see how their position has evolved week by week.

This is gold for content and apps: leaderboards, "biggest movers of the week", race-to-the-finals dashboards, season predictions. All in one place, always up to date.

[Try the rankings endpoints](https://padelapi.org/docs/api-reference/ranking/list-rankings)

## 2. Deeper FIP coverage

We've extended our coverage down the FIP pyramid. **FIP Silver** is now fully tracked alongside Premier Padel, FIP Major, P1, P2, Platinum and Gold — with more low-tier events on the way.

That means more matches, more players, and more story to tell about the rising names climbing the circuit before they reach Premier Padel.

[See tournament coverage](https://padelapi.org/docs#tournaments-coverage)

## 3. Webhooks — say goodbye to polling

The big one: **Webhooks** are live for **tournament**, **match** and **player** events.

Subscribe once, get pushed every change — a new tournament, a corrected result, an updated player profile — the moment it happens. No more polling loops. No more cron jobs. No more wasted requests.

Available on the **Business plan**.

[Learn more about Webhooks](https://padelapi.org/docs/webhooks)
